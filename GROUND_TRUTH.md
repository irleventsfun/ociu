## Core structural distinction

- Tier 1 is the activation, community, and services layer: T1A, T1B, and T1C.
- The 90-day private beta of T1A happens during Phase/Step 2 as a continuation of the Tier 1 program build and validation process.
- The private beta is not the whole of Tier 2.
- Tier 2's permanent offer architecture is Perry's Custom Diagnostic Tool plus SOS, the Business Operating System SaaS.
- During Phase/Step 2, the team tests T1A with selected existing Perry clients while also building and introducing the diagnostic and SOS pathway.
- T1A is sold publicly only after the private beta produces the needed results, proof, feedback, and revisions.

## Phase 1 — Top of pipeline and product creation

Phase 1 begins at the top of the pipeline with:

1. The $1 Million Idea Search.
2. PM in the AM.
3. Creation of the Entrepreneur Manure / EM:Dirt Academy videos.
4. Supporting campaign assets, social content, lead capture, website/data infrastructure, and Chicago launch infrastructure.

The academy videos are created first. The guide, worksheets, and implementation materials are then built backward from the finished teaching experience so the packaged product reflects the program Perry actually wants to deliver.

Phase 1 is both a media-engine build and a product-development build.

## Entrepreneur Manure positioning

Keep Manure in the title.

Working title:

**Entrepreneur Manure: The Dirt on Growing Your Business**

The title is intentionally provocative and acts as a qualification filter.

The audience should not be afraid to:

- Roll up their sleeves.
- Get messy.
- Review basic fundamentals.
- Admit that a plant without fertilizer will not grow.
- Target niche audiences instead of trying to appeal to everyone.
- Test simple content and advertising programs.
- Use AI, Reddit, direct response, social ads, and other practical channels.
- Measure what actually produces growth.

The central metaphor is not grossness for its own sake. It is:

> Plants need fertilizer. Businesses do too.

The program should target businesses and niche audiences where this practical, get-dirty mentality already fits and where straightforward improvements in content, ads, AI, follow-up, and positioning can create major gains.
