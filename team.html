Phase 1 — Build T1A

Create the full attention-to-offer system.

  • $1 Million Idea man-on-the-street format, bumpers, promos, graphics, influencer-share content, and campaign assets.
  • Months of prerecorded PM in the AM episodes plus live-stream audience strategy.
  • EM:Dirt academy built from Perry’s existing books, seminars, modules, and teachings.
  • Every lesson: video, write-up, action, analyze, repeat.
  • Pocket AI, guide, worksheets, accountability, message board, website and social reface, CRM, attribution, and retargeting.
Perry: source material, authority, selected recordings, standards. Team: production, curriculum, build, systems, distribution, and operations.

Phase 2 — Search & Beta

Use the free search to build the pool and improve the product.

  • Run the $1 Million Idea Search for three months.
  • Release the pre-produced man-on-the-street videos and PM in the AM content.
  • Give the first 100 users a more hands-on experience while refining the funnel and tool.
  • Retarget applicants, viewers, and incomplete registrations with the $297 EM:Dirt offer.
  • Use 10 existing Perry clients as experienced quality control for the guide and consistency checklist.
Perry: authority and quality standard. Team: ads, leads, onboarding, support, tracking, interviews, revisions, and case studies.
